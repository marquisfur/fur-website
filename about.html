About Marquis Fur

关于Marquis Fur

Marquis Fur is a professional fur garment manufacturer located in Daying Town, Hengshui Hebei — China’s famous fur industry base.

We specialize in fox fur, raccoon fur, mink fur coats, jackets and vests. We accept OEM & ODM custom orders for worldwide wholesale buyers. From raw fur material selection, cutting, sewing to final inspection, every procedure is controlled in our own factory.

Our advantage: Stable supply, competitive factory price, flexible MOQ and on-time delivery. Welcome to visit our factory.

主营狐狸、貉子、水貂皮大衣、外套、马甲。面向全球外贸批发商承接OEM/ODM定制订单。从原料选皮、裁皮、缝制到成品检验全部自主工厂完成。
